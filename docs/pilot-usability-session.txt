MAKO: THREE BUSINESS OWNER USABILITY SESSIONS

Invite three owners individually for a 20-minute session. Use fictional data and ask before recording. Do not collect passwords or payment information.

Start: "Please think aloud. We are testing Mako, not you. I will let you try before helping."

Without coaching, ask each owner to:
1. Add a fictional customer.
2. Create a task for that customer and set a due date.
3. Create a draft invoice. Do not send it or make a payment.
4. Find today's work on the overview.
5. Find Connections and explain which apps are connected.
6. Find Send feedback and describe one confusing moment.

Record per task: completed unaided / completed with help / not completed; time; where they hesitated; their exact words. Stop a task after three minutes and offer help.

Ask: What would you use tomorrow? What felt unnecessary? What would stop you using this for real work?

Success target: all three can add a customer and task without help; at least two can draft an invoice without help. Prioritize repeated blockers before adding features.
